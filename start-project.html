How to Plan a Commercial Padel Court Project

01

Define Project Requirements

Start by defining the project type — padel club, hotel, sports facility, school or commercial development. Establish the number of courts, intended use, target users and any specific performance requirements. Having a clear project brief at the start reduces revision cycles and helps the manufacturer provide a more accurate proposal. Consider both immediate requirements and potential future expansion.

02

Confirm Site Conditions

Site conditions directly affect court selection, foundation design and overall project cost. Key factors include the available site dimensions, whether the installation is indoor or outdoor, existing ground conditions, access for installation equipment and local climate. For coastal or high-wind sites, structural requirements should be reviewed early. Site photos and drawings help the project team provide a more precise proposal.

03

Select the Padel Court

Court type selection depends on venue type, visibility requirements, environment and budget. The Panoramic Padel Court is the standard commercial choice for most clubs and outdoor projects. The Super Panoramic is suited to premium indoor venues. The Mobile / Modular Court is designed for flexible or leased sites. The FORCE-HX is built for coastal and high-wind environments.

04

Choose the Foundation

Foundation selection depends on the site and project requirements. Most commercial padel courts are installed on an existing concrete base. Where no suitable concrete base exists, a new concrete foundation or a modular steel foundation may be considered. Modular steel foundations are designed for sites where reducing permanent civil work is important, such as leased sites or venues requiring future relocation. Final foundation design should be confirmed by a qualified structural engineer.

05

Evaluate Roof and Weather Requirements

A padel court roof system extends playability in variable weather and is an important consideration for year-round commercial venues. Roof options include fixed and retractable systems. Roof selection should account for local wind loads, structural requirements and the venue's operational priorities. For high-wind or coastal sites, both the court structure and roof system require structural review against local building codes.

06

Prepare Engineering, Delivery and Installation

Once the court scope is confirmed, the project moves into engineering and manufacturing. Technical drawings and documentation are prepared for the project. Shipping is coordinated based on destination port, local import requirements and timeline. Installation support requirements vary by project — some clients manage installation locally, while others require supplier-side technical support. These details are confirmed as part of the project proposal.

To prepare a tailored commercial padel court proposal, the following project details are typically required. You do not need all of these at the initial stage — partial information is fine to start the conversation.

Commercial Padel Project FAQ

How much space is required for a padel court?

A standard padel court playing area is 10 × 20 m. The 20 × 10 m figure describes the internal playing court only. A commercial site needs additional room for the structure, entrances, circulation, maintenance, drainage, lighting and supporting facilities. There is no reliable universal total-site dimension; final clearances should be established through a coordinated project layout and local requirements. See our padel court dimensions guide for full planning specifications.

What information is needed for a padel court quotation?

A commercial padel court quotation typically requires: project country and destination port, number of courts, court type, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor installation, existing foundation condition, roof requirements, target installation timeline, and ideally site photos or drawings. The more detail provided, the more accurate the proposal. Use the inquiry form above to start.

Does a padel court need a concrete foundation?

Most standard padel courts are installed on an existing concrete or paved base. If a suitable concrete base already exists on site, the court can typically be installed directly. If no suitable base exists, a new concrete foundation or a modular steel foundation can be considered depending on site conditions, project type and local soil requirements.

Can a padel court use a modular steel foundation?

Yes. SPORTENVO offers a modular steel foundation system for projects where reducing permanent civil work is a priority — such as leased sites, temporary venues or sites with specific ground conditions. The modular foundation supports standard court installation and allows future relocation. Foundation selection should be confirmed based on site conditions and local structural requirements.

How long does a commercial padel court project take?

Project timeline depends on scope, destination, foundation type, customs clearance and installation resources. Manufacturing and shipping for a standard single-court project typically requires several weeks. Multi-court or complex projects with engineering reviews, customs processing and on-site installation take longer. A project-specific timeline is provided after reviewing the project brief and destination details. See our project planning guides for more detail.

Can a roof be added to a padel court?

Yes. SPORTENVO provides fixed and retractable padel court roof systems for commercial projects. A roof system requires structural review to ensure compatibility with the court system, wind loads and local building requirements. Roof type selection depends on venue type, climate, budget and operational priorities. Roof solutions can be included as part of a complete project package.

What type of padel court is suitable for coastal or high-wind areas?

The FORCE-HX is a reinforced padel court system engineered for high-wind, coastal, hurricane and typhoon-prone project environments. It features a 200 × 200 × 6 mm main structure, 12 mm tempered glass and a wind-resistance design target of up to 136.8 MPH. Final structural requirements must be reviewed according to local building codes, wind conditions, site exposure and foundation design.

Can SPORTENVO provide technical drawings and installation support?

Yes. SPORTENVO provides technical drawings, engineering documentation and project support as part of commercial project packages. The scope of support depends on project size and requirements. Submit a project brief through the planning form above to receive a proposal that includes documentation and support options relevant to your project. Quality and certification details are available on request.

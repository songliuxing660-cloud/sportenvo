Ask the project team ↗
Fixed or retractable roof?

A fixed roof provides permanent overhead coverage. A retractable system adds opening mechanisms and maintenance requirements. Compare both against the venue layout and operating plan.

Does the roof attach to the court?

Court, roof and foundation need a coordinated structural design. Do not assume an existing court frame can support a roof without review.

Is it suitable for coastal sites?

Wind exposure, corrosion protection, drainage and local structural requirements must be assessed for the specific site.

What information is needed?

Send the project location, court layout, available height, ground condition and preferred roof type. Include existing drawings and site photos where available.

What warranty terms apply?

Request written coverage, exclusions and the claim process for the quoted components before placing the order.

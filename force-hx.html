Ask the project team ↗
What makes FORCE-HX different?

The published configuration uses 200 × 200 × 6 mm main structural members, with 12 mm tempered glass and welded mesh. Final specifications depend on the project review.

Is the wind target a site approval?

No. The stated wind-resistance design target does not establish compliance for every location. Confirm applicable loads, exposure, foundation and local requirements with the project engineering team.

Can it be used at a coastal resort?

A coastal project can be evaluated, including wind exposure, corrosion protection, anchoring and maintenance. The location and ground conditions determine the required configuration.

What is needed for a quotation?

Send the country and city, court count, site dimensions, ground details and roof requirements. Include local wind-load information and site drawings if available.

What warranty terms apply?

Request written coverage, exclusions and the claim process for the quoted components before placing the order.

What Is the FORCE-HX High-Wind Padel Court?

FORCE-HX is a reinforced commercial padel court developed for coastal, hurricane, typhoon and high-wind project environments. The system features a 200×200×6 mm main structure, 12 mm tempered glass, reinforced mesh and a wind-resistance design target of up to 136.8 MPH. It is designed for projects where standard commercial court structural performance may not be sufficient for local wind conditions or site exposure.

Engineering note: Final structural requirements must be reviewed according to local building codes, wind conditions, site exposure and foundation design. The wind resistance figure is a design reference target; project-specific structural review is recommended.

Product Overview

136.8 MPH High-Wind Design – Reinforced Structure

The FORCE-HX uses a 200×200×6 mm main structural profile — significantly heavier than a standard commercial court — combined with a 50×50×4 mm reinforced mesh system and 12 mm tempered glass. The system is positioned for projects in coastal, typhoon and hurricane-prone environments where structural performance is the primary buying criterion.

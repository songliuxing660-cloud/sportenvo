Quick answer: a padel court budget has two parts: the court system, which SPORTENVO supplies from USD 9,500 EXW per court, and the local works — freight, foundation, groundworks, drainage and installation — which often cost as much as the court itself. Padel Court Construction Cost FAQ

How much does it cost to build a padel court?

It depends on the court model, foundation, freight and local site works. SPORTENVO court systems start at USD 9,500 EXW per court. In the LTA's 2025 UK guidance, one court with lights plus ring beam, base, ducting and drainage totalled about GBP 71,000, excluding VAT and fees.

What does the calculator include?

It adds SPORTENVO EXW reference prices for the selected court system and optional modular foundation, then adds the freight, local site works and installation costs you enter. It does not include taxes, import duties, permits, electrical connection or design fees.

Why are site works such a large part of the budget?

Groundworks, drainage, the slab or ring beam and lighting ducts are built locally and depend on soil, levels, access and labour rates. In the LTA's UK example, site works for one court cost almost as much as the court with lights.

Is the calculator result a quotation?

No. It is a planning estimate based on published reference prices and your own inputs. Send the site location, court count and drawings to receive a written quotation for your project.

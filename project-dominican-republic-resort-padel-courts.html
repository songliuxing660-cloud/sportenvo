Project Challenges

What shaped the project brief.

01

Landscape integration

The courts needed to sit within tropical greenery while remaining visually open and suitable for a residential racket-club environment.

02

Guest-focused operation

Two courts needed to support casual play, coaching, social sessions and the club's growing padel program alongside its tennis facilities.

03

Tropical outdoor exposure

Sun, humidity and coastal weather conditions make material selection, drainage and maintenance planning important.

Buyer Takeaways

Use this case as a planning reference.

Two courts can be enough to create a real amenity.

For residential racket clubs and community sports venues, a two-court configuration can support social play and programmed activity while preserving landscape and circulation space.

Venue operators should plan aesthetics and operation together.

Frame colour, turf, lighting and court placement influence how the sports facility feels within the wider guest environment.

Outdoor tropical projects still need technical discipline.

Climate, drainage, corrosion protection, UV exposure and local installation responsibilities should be reviewed before final specification.

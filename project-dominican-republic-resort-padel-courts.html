Dominican Republic · Resort / Residential Community

Two panoramic padel courts for a tropical resort setting

Esperanza Racket Club in Las Terrenas uses two Panoramic courts as a leisure and social amenity within a tropical residential-resort environment.

Company Project Reference · Project facts cross-checked against the existing archive

Project Snapshot

A boutique two-court resort configuration.

The project shows how a small court cluster can add guest activity and social value without dominating the surrounding landscape.

ProjectEsperanza Racket Club
LocationLas Terrenas, Dominican Republic
Court Quantity2 courts
Court TypePanoramic
ApplicationLuxury resort / residential community / leisure amenity
Visual ConfigurationRoyal blue turf + black steel frame + transparent glass walls
Project Challenges

What shaped the project brief.

01

Landscape integration

The court needed to sit within tropical greenery while remaining visually open and suitable for a hospitality environment.

02

Guest-focused operation

Two courts needed to support casual play, doubles matches, social clinics and evening use without requiring a large sports complex.

03

Tropical outdoor exposure

Sun, humidity and coastal weather conditions make material selection, drainage and maintenance planning important.

Buyer Takeaways

Use this case as a planning reference.

Two courts can be enough to create a real amenity.

For resorts and residential communities, a two-court configuration can support social play and programmed activity while preserving landscape and circulation space.

Hospitality buyers should plan aesthetics and operation together.

Frame colour, turf, lighting and court placement influence how the sports facility feels within the wider guest environment.

Outdoor resort projects still need technical discipline.

Climate, drainage, corrosion protection, UV exposure and local installation responsibilities should be reviewed before final specification.

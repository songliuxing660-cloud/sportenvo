Thailand · Tropical Climate · Covered Club

Four-court covered padel club project in Chiang Mai

PADEL.CNX combines four panoramic courts, black artificial turf, straight-arm LED lighting and a large steel roof to support year-round operation in a hot, humid and rain-prone climate.

Company Project Reference · Facts cross-checked against the existing project archive

Project Snapshot

A real venue with a clear operating brief.

This reference focuses on the details buyers can reuse when planning a similar site: court quantity, venue conditions, recorded supply scope and project-specific constraints.

Project Challenges

What shaped the final configuration.

01

Heavy rain and intense sun

The venue needed shelter from frequent rain and strong tropical sunlight while maintaining the required court clearance.

02

High humidity

Material selection and the interface between court, lighting and roof had to suit a humid Southeast Asian environment.

03

Night-time club operation

Lighting had to support evening bookings without compromising player visibility or the roof / court layout.

Recorded Project Scope

What the project archive confirms.

01

Panoramic court systems

The archive records four panoramic court systems supplied for the commercial club.

02

Black turf configuration

A black textured monofilament playing surface was selected as part of the club's visual identity.

03

Lighting coordination

Straight-arm LED lighting was coordinated with the court layout for evening operation.

04

Roof interface support

The project record notes coordination of court clearance, roof compatibility, lighting and glass installation under the large steel roof. Buyers planning a separate roof package can use the padel court roof construction guide to compare fixed and retractable systems, foundation loads, wind and drainage, then review the separate SPORTENVO padel court roof systems page for current fixed and retractable project options. The roof shown in this case should not be assumed to be the same configuration as the current roof product range.

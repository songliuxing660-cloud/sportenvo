Quality Control

Checkpoints from material confirmation to packing.

The purpose of quality control is to keep the manufactured court aligned with the approved project scope before it reaches the site.

01

Material Confirmation

Confirm steel, glass, turf, lighting and project-specific component requirements before production.

02

Fabrication Check

Review dimensions, component consistency, connection points and fabrication against the confirmed configuration.

03

Surface Treatment

Check the specified corrosion-protection and coating process for the supplied steel components.

04

Component Matching

Confirm hardware, panels, lighting, turf and accessories are prepared for the same project package.

05

Pre-Shipment Review

Review project identification, major components and available inspection evidence before dispatch.

06

Export Packing

Prepare component protection, packing lists and shipping marks for international delivery and site handling.

Real Manufacturing Evidence

What the factory photographs actually show.

These are real production photographs supplied for SPORTENVO's website. They provide visible evidence of component handling and fabrication activity; they are not used as a substitute for order-specific material certificates, test reports or inspection records.

Engineering & manufacturing →
Evidence boundary. These photographs can support the existence of visible fabrication, finishing, glass handling and component staging activities. They do not by themselves verify steel grade, coating thickness, welding standard, glass compliance, certification status or the specification of a specific order. Those items should be confirmed through the quotation and applicable project documents.
Project Documentation

Ask for documents tied to the actual order.

A logo or certificate badge alone does not define the supplied court. The relevant document package depends on the product, destination and project scope.

Technical Drawings

Court dimensions, interfaces, anchors and assembly information as applicable.

Specification Confirmation

Written confirmation of the materials and components included in the quotation.

Inspection Evidence

Production or pre-shipment inspection records and photographs where available.

Packing Information

Packing list, shipping marks and component identification for site handling.

Installation Documents

Assembly sequence, installation guidance and supplier / local responsibility boundaries.

Certification Support

Available product documentation should be checked against the exact supplied configuration and destination requirements.

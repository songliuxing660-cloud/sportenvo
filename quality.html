SPORTENVO · Buyer Verification

Project evidence.
Not just product claims.

Use this page to verify what can be seen publicly, what should be confirmed in a quotation, what can be tied to an actual order and what still requires local project engineering. The purpose is to make commercial padel court procurement easier to audit before production and delivery.

Evidence Framework

Know what each type of evidence can actually prove.

Commercial buyers should separate public photos, written quotation scope, order-specific records and local engineering approval. They answer different questions and should not be treated as interchangeable proof.

Buyer rule: a website image can show visible activity. It cannot by itself prove steel grade, coating thickness, glass compliance, structural suitability or the specification of your exact order.

Level 01 · Public

Visible Evidence

Factory-floor photos, installation images, completed projects, published product specifications and official SPORTENVO channels.

Level 02 · Quotation

Written Scope

Selected court system, included components, technical specification, exclusions, foundation / roof interfaces, packing and support responsibilities.

Level 03 · Order

Order-Specific Records

Approved drawings, component identification, available inspection photos, packing information and applicable material / component documents tied to the confirmed order.

Level 04 · Site

Local Engineering

Soil, anchors, wind basis, foundations, building interfaces, permits and locally required approvals remain dependent on the real project location.

03 · Quality Control & Packing

Follow the project from confirmed material scope to export packing.

Quality control is most useful when each checkpoint is tied to the approved project rather than treated as a generic factory promise.

For an actual order, ask which inspection records, photographs, packing records and component checks can be tied to your project number or production batch.

Material confirmation

Confirm steel, glass, turf, lighting, finishes and project-specific options before production.

Fabrication check

Review dimensions, connection points, component consistency and model-specific fabrication requirements.

Surface-treatment check

Confirm the specified corrosion-protection and finishing process for the supplied steel components.

Component matching

Keep court structure, hardware, panels, lighting, turf and accessories tied to the same project package.

Pre-shipment review

Confirm major supplied components, project identification and available inspection evidence before dispatch.

Export packing

Protect glass and structural components, identify packages and prepare packing information for international transport and site handling.

04 · Project Documentation

Ask for documents tied to the actual quotation and order.

A certificate badge, catalogue or marketing statement is not the same as a project document package. Buyers should confirm which documents apply to the quoted configuration and destination.

Document availability varies by product, component and destination. The list below is a procurement checklist, not a claim that every document automatically applies to every project.

01 · Drawings

Technical Drawings

Court dimensions, assembly, anchor / base interface, component positions and roof / foundation interfaces where applicable.

02 · Specification

Written Component Schedule

Model-specific materials and supplied components matched to the commercial quotation rather than a generic catalogue page.

03 · Materials

Material / Component Records

Available steel, glass, lighting or other component records should be matched to the exact supplied item and report scope.

04 · Inspection

QC / Pre-Shipment Evidence

Inspection photographs, records or checklists are most useful when they are identifiable to the actual order or batch.

05 · Packing

Packing & Shipping Information

Packing list, component identification and shipping marks help receiving teams check packages and prepare installation logistics.

06 · Compliance

Applicable Compliance Documents

FIP rules references, CE-related documentation, EN 12150 glass evidence, RoHS lighting information or other reports should only be relied on when their scope matches the quoted product / component.

Verification rule. Before relying on any certificate or test report, confirm the issuer, document number, date, tested product or component, applicable standard and whether the report actually covers the configuration quoted for your project.
05 · Technical Drawings & Site Inputs

The drawing package is where product supply meets the real site.

Commercial projects need more than product dimensions. Court, foundation, roof, drainage, wind exposure, access and local construction responsibilities should be coordinated before site work is fixed.

07 · External Verification

Separate venue verification from supplier verification.

Third-party sources can establish that a venue exists, how many courts it operates and how the facility is described publicly. They should not be used to imply a SPORTENVO supply relationship unless the venue or an independent source explicitly confirms it.

Design Guide

Padel Court Dimensions & Site Planning

Court dimensions are only the starting point. Commercial sites also need circulation, access, installation space and operational planning.

A standard court footprint should be planned together with surrounding space, player circulation, maintenance access and the number of courts planned for the venue.

Plan beyond the playing box

Multi-court clubs need comfortable walkways, entry zones and space for maintenance and operations. The standard Panoramic Padel Court footprint is 10 × 20 m, but site planning should account for perimeter clearance, access and any foundation or roof system requirements.

Check height and indoor clearances

Indoor venues need sufficient clear height and structural coordination around lighting, roof beams and services. See our commercial padel court systems for individual height and structural specifications.

Share a site plan early

A simple marked-up drawing with dimensions can help identify layout constraints before a final court configuration is selected. If your site has uneven ground or is a leased site, the modular padel court foundation system provides height adjustment up to 180 mm.

Project note

Final specifications should be confirmed against the project location, local requirements and site conditions. Use this guide as a planning framework rather than a substitute for project engineering.

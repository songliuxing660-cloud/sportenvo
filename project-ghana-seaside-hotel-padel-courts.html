Ghana · Seaside Hospitality Project

Two-court seaside hotel padel project in Ghana

A hospitality project near the Ghanaian coast added two padel courts as a guest amenity, creating a useful reference for resort buyers planning sport in a salt-air, high-UV environment.

Company Project Reference · Facts cross-checked against the existing project archive

Project Snapshot

A real venue with a clear operating brief.

This reference focuses on the details buyers can reuse when planning a similar site: court quantity, venue conditions, recorded supply scope and project-specific constraints.

Venue TypeSeaside hotel / resort
Project AreaGomoa Fetteh, Ghana
Court Quantity2 padel courts
EnvironmentCoastal / sea breeze / strong UV / moisture exposure
InstallationCompleted by the local team with technical guidance
Project Challenges

What shaped the final configuration.

01

Coastal corrosion exposure

Sea breeze and moisture increase the importance of corrosion protection, coating quality and maintenance planning.

02

Hospitality integration

The courts needed to add recreational value without overpowering the resort environment.

03

Outdoor durability

UV exposure, weather and year-round outdoor use needed to be considered in the supplied configuration.

Buyer Takeaways

What a buyer can learn from this site.

Design from the venue outward.

Climate, site circulation, court quantity and operating hours should shape the system before a quotation is finalised.

Separate equipment from local works.

Define the court package, roof or foundation interfaces, local construction and installation responsibilities clearly.

Use real projects for due diligence.

Compare a reference with your own venue type, environment, scale and operating model rather than relying on generic product images.

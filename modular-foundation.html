Ask the project team ↗
Can it replace concrete on every site?

No. A modular steel base is an alternative for suitable ground conditions. Drainage, support capacity, anchoring and local requirements still need review.

How is the base leveled?

Adjustable supports are leveled to the approved layout. The published configuration allows adjustment up to 180 mm; the actual site determines suitability.

Can it support a roof?

A roof changes structural and foundation loads. Assess the roof, court and base together; do not assume the court foundation also supports a roof.

What should I send for review?

Provide the location, dimensions, ground photos, level differences, drainage information and planned court count. Include any existing foundation drawings.

What warranty terms apply?

Request written coverage, exclusions and the claim process for the quoted components before placing the order.

MODULAR FOUNDATION SYSTEM

Padel Court Modular Foundation Base

Quick Answer: Does a padel court need concrete? Not every suitable project requires a full permanent concrete slab. An engineered modular steel foundation can reduce dependence on traditional concrete work, but the site still needs competent, level preparation plus drainage, load, anchoring, wind and local engineering review. The correct foundation remains specific to the ground and court system.

System Overview

Designed around real project conditions.

Modular steel foundation system designed to reduce permanent civil works and improve installation flexibility.

The padel court modular foundation is specified around venue use, local climate, ground conditions and the confirmed commercial brief. Final structural and foundation requirements remain project-specific.

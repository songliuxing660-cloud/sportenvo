Complete Project System

Court, Foundation and Roof as One Project

Most commercial padel projects require more than a court structure. The panoramic padel court is the most widely used starting point for clubs and resorts. Projects on leased or relocatable sites typically combine the mobile padel court with the modular padel court foundation to avoid permanent civil works. Coastal and high-wind environments require the FORCE-HX high-wind padel court, which is engineered specifically for extreme weather exposure. For clubs seeking year-round operation, a padel court roof system — fixed or retractable — can be combined with any court type as part of a coordinated project scope.

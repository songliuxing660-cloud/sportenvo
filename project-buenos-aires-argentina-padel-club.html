Argentina · Urban Multi-Court Club

Five-court panoramic padel club project in Buenos Aires

Conecta Padel uses a 4 indoor + 1 outdoor court mix to balance weather reliability, peak-hour capacity and an outdoor player experience within a compact urban club.

Company Project Reference · Facts cross-checked against the existing project archive

A real venue with a clear operating brief.

Project Challenges

What shaped the final configuration.

01

Indoor + outdoor in one club

The covered and exposed courts required different planning for structure, drainage, weather exposure and operating use.

02

Compact urban site

Court spacing, central circulation and spectator movement needed to work efficiently within a neighbourhood setting.

03

Peak-hour capacity

Four covered courts provide dependable booking capacity while the outdoor court adds flexibility when weather allows.

Recorded Project Scope

What the project archive confirms.

01

Five panoramic courts

The company project archive records five Panoramic systems in the finished club.

02

Layout planning

The indoor court spacing, central walkway and player / spectator circulation were considered as one facility.

03

Export coordination

Production, container loading, export documentation and delivery were coordinated against the construction schedule.

04

Installation support

Technical drawings and remote guidance were provided to support the local installation team.

Project Planning · Commercial Buyer Guide

How Much Does a Padel Court Cost?

Quick Answer: There is no single universal padel court price. Final project cost depends on the selected court system, foundation type, glass, turf, LED lighting, optional roof, shipping destination, installation scope and local site conditions. A standard commercial court, a mobile court with modular foundation, a high-wind reinforced system or a court with a retractable roof can have very different project budgets. Panoramic, Super Panoramic, Mobile and FORCE-HX systems each have different structural scope. For an accurate quotation, buyers should provide project location, court quantity, preferred model, indoor or outdoor setting, foundation and roof requirements.

Padel Court Model and Structure

Different padel court models have different structural specifications and project scopes. The court model is usually the starting point for any project cost discussion.

  • Panoramic Padel Court — The most widely used commercial court model. Combines 12 mm tempered glass, galvanized steel structure, professional turf and LED lighting. Suitable for outdoor clubs, sports centers, resorts and indoor venues.
  • Super Panoramic Padel Court — Premium visual concept with greater glass openness. Often selected for high-end clubs and showcase venues. Structural and material scope differs from the standard panoramic system.
  • Mobile / Modular Padel Court — Integrates the padel court with a modular steel foundation system. Relevant for leased sites, temporary projects and venues where reducing permanent civil work is important. Foundation system adds to the total project scope.
  • FORCE-HX High-Wind Padel Court — Reinforced 200 × 200 × 6 mm main structure designed for coastal, hurricane-prone and typhoon-exposed projects. Structural specification is heavier than a standard court, which affects material and engineering scope.
  • Padel Court with Roof — Any court model combined with a fixed or retractable roof system. Roof adds significant project scope, structural requirements and installation complexity.

How Foundation Type Affects Padel Court Cost

Foundation represents a significant variable in padel court project budgets. Two main approaches serve most commercial projects, each with different implications for cost, civil work and project flexibility.

Traditional ConcreteModular Steel Foundation
Construction methodConcrete pour with formworkPrefabricated steel modules
Civil work requiredYes — excavation, pour, cureReduced — assembly and leveling
Leveling flexibilityFixed once setUp to 180 mm adjustable height
Relocation potentialLow — permanent workHigher — bolted connections

Do not assume the modular foundation is always less expensive. It depends on local civil-work pricing, site conditions and engineering requirements. Review the modular padel court foundation system and the modular foundation planning guide to understand when each approach is appropriate.

Glass, Steel, Turf and Lighting Cost Factors

The core court components differ between models and configurations. Buyers should confirm what is included in each quote before comparing prices.

  • Tempered Glass: 12 mm tempered safety glass is standard in panoramic and super panoramic courts. Glass thickness, fixing method and installation requirements affect overall scope.
  • Steel Structure: Steel section size, galvanizing treatment, powder coating and surface finish differ between standard and high-wind models. FORCE-HX uses a heavier 200 × 200 × 6 mm main beam compared with standard sections.
  • Artificial Turf: Padel turf grade, pile height, material and infill can vary. Higher-specification turf contributes to the overall playing system cost.
  • LED Lighting: Lighting wattage, IP rating, brand and quantity affect both the initial supply cost and long-term energy consumption. Confirm the LED specification and layout when reviewing a quotation.
  • Surface Treatment: Hot-dip galvanizing, powder coating and corrosion protection for coastal projects add to the structural supply cost.

How a Roof Changes the Project Budget

Adding a fixed or retractable roof to a padel court project substantially increases total project scope and budget. Key considerations include:

  • Roof type: Fixed roofs are structurally simpler. Retractable roofs provide operational flexibility but require more complex engineering and mechanical systems.
  • Roof size: Coverage for one or multiple courts increases material, structural and installation cost proportionally.
  • Structural support: Roof columns, connections and foundation design must accommodate the roof loads in addition to court loads.
  • Membrane material: PVDF or similar technical membrane adds to the material scope.
  • Wind load: Exposed locations increase structural and anchoring requirements for the roof.
  • Drainage: Roof drainage design and installation add to project scope.

Review the padel court roof system and the padel court roof guide before estimating the budget impact of a roof.

Shipping Cost

International shipping is a project-specific variable that cannot be quoted without destination information. Key factors include:

  • Destination country and port of entry
  • Number of courts and packaging volume
  • Container type and utilization
  • Project schedule and shipping timeline
  • Local customs duties and inland delivery requirements

Shipping should be requested as a line item at quotation stage, not assumed from a price list. Multi-court orders typically benefit from better container utilization relative to single-court projects.

Installation Cost

Installation cost is location-dependent and varies significantly by country and project. Buyers typically use one of the following models:

  • Buyer's local team: Buyer contracts local installation using SPORTENVO technical drawings and guidance.
  • Local specialist contractor: A padel court installer engaged by the buyer, working from approved technical documentation.
  • Project-specific technical support: SPORTENVO can provide installation guidance and support depending on project location, scope and arrangements confirmed at quotation stage.

Labor cost, access conditions, team travel and local regulations all vary by market. Confirm the installation model at quotation stage rather than assuming it is included in the equipment price.

Indoor vs Outdoor Padel Court Cost

Indoor and outdoor projects have different project requirements, which affect the total cost scope.

Outdoor projects may require additional considerations for: wind exposure and structural loading, drainage from the playing surface, corrosion protection in coastal or humid environments, foundation design for external conditions, and weather protection via a roof system if required.

Indoor projects may involve: building ceiling height and access constraints, coordination with existing floor or foundation systems, integration with building electrical systems, and potential structural review by the building owner. Review padel court dimensions and site planning early for both indoor and outdoor projects.

Neither application is automatically more or less expensive. The total project cost depends on the specific conditions at the site.

What Information Is Needed for an Accurate Padel Court Quote?

Providing complete project information at the start of the enquiry allows a more accurate and comparable quotation. Prepare the following:

  1. Project country and city — affects shipping, customs, engineering and local requirements
  2. Number of courts — quantity affects material planning, packing and shipping efficiency
  3. Indoor or outdoor application — determines structural, foundation and weather-protection requirements
  4. Preferred court model — Panoramic, Super Panoramic, Mobile, FORCE-HX or other
  5. Existing ground or site condition — soil type, level, drainage, existing structure
  6. Foundation requirements — traditional concrete or modular steel
  7. Roof requirement — fixed, retractable or none
  8. Color and customization — non-standard color or configuration
  9. Shipping destination or port — nearest port and inland delivery needs
  10. Target project schedule — production and installation timeline

Padel Court Cost FAQ

How much does a padel court cost?

There is no single universal padel court price. Project cost depends on court model, foundation, glass, turf, lighting, optional roof, shipping destination, installation scope and site conditions. SPORTENVO provides project-based B2B quotations. To receive an accurate cost estimate, share your project country, court quantity, preferred model and site information.

Why do padel court prices vary?

Padel court prices vary because project requirements vary. Court model, structural specification, foundation type, roof, shipping destination, installation scope and local conditions all affect the total cost. Comparing quotes without a matching specification and scope is not a reliable basis for budget planning.

Does the quotation include installation?

Installation scope varies by project. Buyers may use a local team, a local specialist contractor, or arrange project-specific support with SPORTENVO. Technical drawings and installation guidance are provided as standard. On-site support depends on project location and arrangements made at quotation stage.

Does the quotation include shipping?

Shipping can be included or excluded depending on the quotation scope. International shipping varies by destination, quantity, packaging volume, container type and schedule. Request shipping as a line item and confirm the destination port and inland delivery requirements when requesting a quotation.

How does the foundation affect the project cost?

Foundation type, civil works, leveling, drainage, anchoring and local engineering requirements significantly affect total project cost. A modular steel foundation reduces dependence on permanent concrete work for selected projects but has its own material and engineering costs. Foundation requirements must be reviewed according to the project site.

Does a padel court roof increase the cost?

Yes. A padel court roof system adds project scope including structural support, membrane, roof foundation requirements, drainage and installation. Fixed and retractable options have different complexity and cost profiles. Review the roof system requirements before including a roof in the budget.

Is a mobile padel court priced differently from a fixed court?

Yes. A mobile padel court includes the court system and the modular steel foundation system, which adds its own material and installation scope. The total cost depends on both the court model and the foundation configuration selected for the specific project.

What information is required to receive an accurate quotation?

Provide: project country and city, number of courts, indoor or outdoor application, preferred court model, site ground condition, foundation type, roof requirement, color preferences, shipping destination and target project schedule.

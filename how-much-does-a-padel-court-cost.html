Project Planning

How Much Does a Padel Court Cost?

Cost depends on court model, foundation, roof, shipping, installation scope and local site conditions.

Cost is best treated as a project range, not a single universal number. A commercial padel project normally combines the court system with site work, delivery and optional infrastructure.

Start with the site and operating model

A leased site may favor a modular padel court foundation and a mobile & modular padel court, while a permanent club can justify conventional civil works. Indoor venues may not need a roof, while outdoor commercial sites may value weather protection.

Separate equipment from project cost

Compare quotes by scope: steel structure, tempered glass, turf, lighting, accessories, foundation, roof, logistics, installation support and any local construction. See our range of commercial padel court systems for baseline specification reference.

Use a comparable specification

The lowest headline price is not useful if the structural system, surface treatment, glass, lighting or included services are different. The Panoramic Padel Court is a widely used baseline for many club and resort projects. Build a comparison sheet before deciding.

Project note

Final specifications should be confirmed against the project location, local requirements and site conditions. Use this guide as a planning framework rather than a substitute for project engineering.

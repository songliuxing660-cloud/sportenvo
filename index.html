FAQ

Padel Court Project FAQ

What does a commercial padel court include?

A commercial padel court includes a steel frame structure, 12 mm tempered glass back and side walls, welded wire mesh panels, artificial padel turf, LED lighting, net and posts, and surface markings. Depending on the project, the package may also include foundation systems, roof structures, technical drawings and installation documentation.

How much space is required for a padel court?

The standard padel court playing area is 10 × 20 metres. Commercial projects require additional space beyond the playing area for structural installation, access, maintenance circulation, drainage and local safety or planning requirements. Total site area depends on the number of courts, layout and local regulations.

Does a padel court need a concrete foundation?

Standard padel court installations commonly require a suitable concrete or paved base to anchor the court structure. For selected projects on leased sites or where permanent construction needs to be reduced, the SPORTENVO modular steel foundation may be considered depending on site conditions and engineering review.

Can a padel court be installed on a modular foundation?

SPORTENVO's modular steel foundation provides an alternative installation option for selected leased sites, temporary venues and projects where permanent civil work needs to be minimised. Final suitability depends on site conditions, load requirements and local engineering assessment.

Which padel court is suitable for high-wind areas?

The FORCE-HX padel court is designed for coastal, high-wind, hurricane and typhoon-prone project environments. It features a 200 × 200 × 6 mm main structure and a wind-resistance design target of up to 136.8 MPH. Final structural requirements depend on local building codes, wind conditions and site exposure.

Can SPORTENVO supply the court, foundation and roof together?

SPORTENVO provides complete padel project packages including court systems, modular foundation options, fixed and retractable roof systems, technical drawings, manufacturing and project support. Projects can be configured by court type, quantity, site conditions, weather requirements and delivery destination.

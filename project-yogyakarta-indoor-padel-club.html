Indonesia · Premium Indoor Club

Premium indoor padel club project in Yogyakarta

The Circles Club combines four Super Panoramic courts with café, wellness and social spaces as a premium indoor lifestyle venue.

Company Project Reference · Verified against the existing project archive

Project Snapshot

A commercial venue shaped by its operating model.

This case study focuses on the project facts that matter to buyers: court mix, venue conditions, recorded supply scope and the planning decisions behind the finished facility.

ProjectThe Circles Club
LocationYogyakarta, Indonesia
Venue TypePremium indoor sports and social club
Court Quantity4 courts
Court Mix4 Super Panoramic courts
Venue FeaturesRestaurant / health bar, wellness functions and social space
Project Challenges

What had to be solved before delivery.

01

Premium visual positioning

The court system had to support a high-end club identity, not simply provide a playable enclosure.

02

Indoor integration

Court height, lighting and the high-ceiling steel structure had to work together for safe play and a clean venue experience.

03

Cross-border procurement

The investor required predictable manufacturing, packing, shipping and technical-document support for delivery to Indonesia.

Recorded Supply Scope

What the project package included.

01

Super Panoramic courts

Four open-view Super Panoramic courts form the club's premium playing configuration.

02

Consistent four-court layout

All four courts are publicly presented as Super Panoramic, giving the venue a consistent open-view visual language.

03

Project-specific configuration

The recorded solution coordinated the court systems with indoor roof height, lighting and venue aesthetics.

04

Export project support

The project archive records factory supply, professional packing, international shipping and technical-document support.

Buyer Takeaways

Use this project as a planning reference.

Start with the operating model.

Court quantity, court mix and covered / uncovered areas should reflect bookings, coaching, events and the target customer experience.

Define the site interfaces.

Confirm roof clearance, ground or slab condition, access, drainage, utilities and local installation responsibilities before manufacturing.

Compare the complete scope.

Evaluate courts, technical documents, packing, logistics and installation support together rather than comparing a single headline equipment price.

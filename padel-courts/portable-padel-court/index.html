FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the difference between a portable padel court and a normal padel court?

The main difference is the foundation system. A portable padel court can use a modular steel base, while a traditional court is normally installed on a permanent concrete foundation. The main court structure — tempered glass, steel posts, turf and LED lighting — remains the same.

Does a portable padel court require a foundation?

Yes. Every professional padel court requires a stable and properly prepared base. A modular foundation can reduce certain civil works for suitable sites, but it does not eliminate the need for site preparation and structural evaluation.

Can the padel court be relocated?

A modular system can provide greater relocation flexibility than a traditional permanent foundation. Actual relocation feasibility depends on the site, installation method and project configuration.

Is the system suitable for commercial padel clubs?

Yes. The system is designed for commercial applications including padel clubs, sports centers, hotels, resorts and multi-court projects.

What is included in a complete portable padel court?

A complete system can include steel structure, mesh panels, 12 mm tempered glass, artificial turf, LED lighting, modular foundation components and installation hardware.

Can the court color be customized?

Yes. Steel structure color, turf color and selected visual elements can be customized according to project requirements.

Can you supply several courts for one commercial project?

Yes. Single-court and multi-court configurations can be planned according to site dimensions and commercial requirements.

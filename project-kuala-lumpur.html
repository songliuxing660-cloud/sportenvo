Project-Specific Considerations

Rooftop padel court installations involve a range of site-specific planning factors that differ from conventional ground-level projects. These were considerations relevant to the Kuala Lumpur project context.

Structural Interface

The court structure must interface correctly with the existing rooftop surface, including load distribution and anchoring to the building structure.

Access and Logistics

Rooftop sites require planning for vertical transport of court components, including steel sections, glass panels and other court elements.

Wind Exposure

Rooftop locations may be exposed to higher wind conditions than sheltered ground-level sites, which should be reviewed as part of the structural and anchoring assessment.

Drainage

Surface drainage from the court must be coordinated with the existing rooftop drainage system to prevent water accumulation.

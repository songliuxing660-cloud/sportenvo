Commercial Padel Courts Planned Around the Club

Quick Answer: What should be planned for a commercial padel club? Start with market and operating model, then confirm site dimensions, court quantity, player circulation, foundation, drainage, lighting, weather protection, logistics and installation responsibilities. Court selection should follow the venue plan rather than being treated as a standalone equipment purchase.

Hospitality Buyer FAQ

Padel court questions for hotels and resorts.

Which padel court is most suitable for a luxury hotel or resort?

Panoramic and Super Panoramic systems are often considered when visual quality and guest-facing presentation are important. The final choice should still follow climate, foundation, maintenance, budget and property design requirements.

How close should a padel court be to guest rooms?

There is no universal distance. Noise, local regulations, property layout, orientation, barriers and operating hours should be reviewed as part of the site plan before the court position is fixed.

What changes for a seaside or island resort?

Coastal projects require extra attention to corrosion protection, wind exposure, logistics, access, drainage and maintenance. Unusual access can also affect packing, lifting and installation sequencing.

Should a resort choose an Electric Tent or a permanent Roof?

Use the operating model to decide. Electric Tent supports retractable open / closed use, while a permanent Roof provides continuous structural coverage. Climate, clear height, drainage, foundations and local approvals should be reviewed for either option.

Can the court colors and finishes match the hotel design?

Selected colors, finishes, turf and lighting can be coordinated within the confirmed production and engineering scope. Structural changes require technical review.

What information should a hotel or resort send before quotation?

Send the project location, site plan, court quantity, guest-area context, climate exposure, foundation status, access limitations, desired finish, weather-protection needs and target opening date.

Project Workflow

From Project Requirement to Court Solution

SPORTENVO's typical B2B project process moves from initial site review to final court delivery.

01

Project Information

Venue type, court quantity, indoor or outdoor, site dimensions, ground condition, climate and roof requirements.

02

Site & Venue Review

Foundation suitability, wind exposure, drainage, access and local engineering requirements.

03

Court System Selection

Panoramic, Super Panoramic, Mobile, FORCE-HX or project-specific configuration confirmed.

04

Technical Configuration

Steel, glass, turf, lighting, foundation system, roof and project-specific drawings confirmed.

05

Production & Shipping

Factory production and export packaging to the confirmed destination port.

06

Installation & Delivery

Installation guidance, technical drawings and project support through to court handover.

Planning a Similar Padel Project?

To receive a project-based quotation or case study reference, prepare the following information:

Project country and city — shipping, climate and local codes
Number of courts — one court or multi-court layout
Indoor or outdoor application — structural and drainage implications
Available site dimensions — including any boundary constraints
Preferred court model — Panoramic, Mobile, FORCE-HX or other
Existing ground / foundation condition — soil, slab or rooftop
Roof requirement — fixed, retractable or none
Local wind / environmental conditions — coastal or exposed locations
Target project schedule — production and installation timeline

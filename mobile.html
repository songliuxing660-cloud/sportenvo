Ask the project team ↗
Does it need a concrete foundation?

A modular steel base can reduce traditional foundation work on suitable sites. It does not remove the need to review ground capacity, drainage, anchoring and local requirements.

Can I relocate the court?

The system may support dismantling and reassembly. Suitability depends on the original configuration, component condition and the new site; the court is not moved intact.

What does the package include?

The agreed package can cover the modular base, court structure, glass, turf, lighting, net system and technical guidance. Confirm each item and local works in the quotation.

What should I send for a site review?

Send the location, court count, dimensions, surface photos, level differences and planned opening date. Include lease or access restrictions where relevant.

What warranty terms apply?

Request written coverage, exclusions and the claim process for the quoted components before placing the order.

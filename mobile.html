What Is a Mobile Padel Court?

A mobile padel court combines a professional court system with a modular steel foundation. It is designed for selected leased sites, commercial clubs and relocatable projects where reduced permanent civil work and future site flexibility are important. The modular padel court foundation uses a steel module system that can be installed with reduced dependence on a conventional permanent concrete slab, depending on site conditions and project requirements.

How It Works

How the Modular Steel Foundation Works

The modular foundation system replaces part of the conventional civil work with a prefabricated steel module assembly. Each module connects to form the court base, with adjustable legs to compensate for minor ground-level variation. The system is designed to reduce on-site construction time compared to a standard poured concrete foundation approach.

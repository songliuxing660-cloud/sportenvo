FAQ

Common questions.

What is a panoramic padel court?

A panoramic padel court uses tempered glass on all main playing surfaces — sides, back walls and corners — to provide maximum visibility and an open playing environment. It is the most common professional court format for commercial padel clubs.

What glass thickness is used?

The court uses 12 mm tempered safety glass that meets EN12150 European standard. This thickness provides the required safety and playing characteristics for a professional commercial padel court.

Is the steel structure galvanized?

Yes. The Q235B steel structure is hot-dip galvanized and powder coated to provide strong corrosion resistance for outdoor commercial use.

Can the court color be customized?

Yes. Steel structure color can be specified from the RAL palette. Turf color and selected court elements can also be customized according to project requirements.

What foundation does the panoramic court need?

The panoramic padel court is normally installed on a prepared concrete foundation. Site conditions, drainage and local engineering requirements should be evaluated before installation. Technical drawings are provided for each project.

Can you supply multi-court projects?

Yes. Single-court and multi-court configurations are available. Multi-court projects can include shared infrastructure, uniform branding and coordinated installation planning.

Do you provide installation support?

Yes. Technical drawings, installation guides and project-specific support are available. Our team can assist with layout planning, foundation requirements and installation preparation.

What Is a Panoramic Padel Court?

A panoramic padel court replaces a portion of the opaque side panels used in more traditional enclosed court designs with large tempered glass sections. This provides clear sightlines for spectators and players around the full playing enclosure, making it the standard system for commercial padel clubs and professional venues.

SPORTENVO panoramic courts use 12 mm tempered glass, Q235B galvanized steel, welded mesh, professional padel turf and 200W IP65 LED lighting. Review the padel court dimensions and site planning guide to prepare your space requirements before requesting a quote.

Final court configuration, foundation and structural requirements should be selected according to the project site, local regulations and environmental conditions.

Foundation

What Foundation Does a Panoramic Padel Court Need?

A panoramic padel court normally requires a stable, level foundation capable of supporting the court structure and maintaining the required playing surface. For permanent commercial projects, a properly designed concrete or paved foundation is commonly used.

For selected leased sites or projects where permanent civil work needs to be reduced, SPORTENVO's modular steel foundation may be considered after site and engineering review.

FAQ

Panoramic Padel Court FAQ

What is a panoramic padel court?

A panoramic padel court is a professional padel court designed with large tempered glass viewing areas to provide clear visibility around the playing enclosure. The system uses 12 mm tempered glass, galvanized steel structure, welded mesh, artificial turf and LED lighting for commercial clubs, sports centers and outdoor padel projects.

What is the difference between panoramic and super panoramic padel courts?

Both use tempered glass for clear visibility. A super panoramic court uses a more open structural design with less steel framing, providing a larger glass area. Panoramic courts suit commercial club and outdoor projects. Super panoramic systems are often chosen for premium clubs and showcase venues. See the full comparison guide.

Is a panoramic padel court suitable for outdoor installation?

Yes. Panoramic padel courts are widely used for outdoor commercial projects. The steel treatment, foundation, anchoring and structural requirements should be confirmed according to the project climate, wind exposure and local engineering requirements.

What glass is used in a panoramic padel court?

12 mm tempered safety glass. It provides the transparent wall system required for panoramic padel court design while maintaining clear visibility for players and spectators. Installation and fixing must follow approved project drawings and local requirements.

Can court colors, turf and lighting be customized?

Yes. Project-specific colors, turf options and lighting configurations can be discussed during the quotation process. Customization options depend on project requirements, quantities and confirmed specifications.

What information is required before quotation?

Project country, number of courts, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor application, foundation condition, roof requirements and target timeline. Site photos or drawings are helpful. These details allow SPORTENVO to confirm the most suitable configuration. Start your project enquiry →

Can SPORTENVO support installation?

Technical drawings and installation guidance are included in the standard supply scope. On-site installation support depends on project location and scope. Installation arrangements should be confirmed during the quotation process.

Is the panoramic court suitable for commercial padel clubs?

Yes. The panoramic padel court is widely used for commercial clubs requiring a professional court system with clear sightlines. It is also used for sports centers, hotels, resorts and commercial developments. View completed panoramic padel court projects for reference.

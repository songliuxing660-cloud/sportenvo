What Is a Panoramic Padel Court?

A panoramic padel court is a professional padel court system designed with large tempered glass viewing areas to provide clear visibility around the playing area. SPORTENVO panoramic courts combine 12 mm tempered glass, galvanized steel structure, welded mesh, professional artificial turf and LED lighting for commercial clubs, resorts, sports centers and outdoor padel projects.

The final court configuration, foundation and structural requirements should be selected according to the project site, local regulations and environmental conditions.

Foundation

What Foundation Does a Panoramic Padel Court Need?

A panoramic padel court normally requires a stable, level foundation capable of supporting the court structure and maintaining the required playing surface. For permanent commercial projects, a properly designed concrete or paved foundation is commonly used.

For selected leased sites or projects where permanent civil work needs to be reduced, SPORTENVO's modular steel foundation may be considered after site and engineering review.

Roof Solutions

Can a Panoramic Padel Court Be Covered?

Explore Padel Court Roof Systems →

Yes. Panoramic padel courts can be combined with selected fixed or retractable roof systems for projects requiring weather protection or improved year-round usability. Roof design must consider court layout, local wind conditions, structural loads, drainage, project dimensions and local building requirements.

Adding a roof to an existing court requires a separate structural engineering review based on the specific project and site conditions.

FAQ

Panoramic Padel Court FAQ

What is a panoramic padel court?

A panoramic padel court is a professional padel court designed with large tempered glass viewing areas around the playing enclosure. SPORTENVO panoramic courts combine 12 mm tempered glass, galvanized steel structure, welded mesh, artificial turf and LED lighting for commercial clubs, resorts and sports facilities.

What glass is used in a panoramic padel court?

SPORTENVO panoramic padel courts use 12 mm tempered glass as part of the playing enclosure. The glass provides the transparent wall system required for padel while maintaining clear visibility for players and spectators. Installation and fixing details should follow the approved project drawings and local requirements.

Is a panoramic padel court suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. Panoramic padel courts are commonly used for outdoor commercial projects. The final steel treatment, foundation, anchoring and structural requirements should be selected according to the project climate, wind conditions, site exposure and local regulations.

What is included in a complete panoramic padel court?

A complete SPORTENVO panoramic padel court can include the galvanized steel structure, 12 mm tempered glass, welded mesh, professional padel turf, LED lighting, net system, structural components, technical drawings, export packing and installation guidance. Optional foundation and roof systems can be configured separately.

What foundation does a panoramic padel court need?

A panoramic padel court requires a stable and level foundation. Permanent commercial projects commonly use a suitable concrete or paved base. A modular steel foundation may be considered for selected projects after reviewing the site and engineering requirements.

What is the difference between panoramic and super panoramic?

Both use tempered glass for clear court visibility, but a super panoramic court generally uses a more open structural design. Panoramic courts are widely used for commercial club projects, while super panoramic systems are often selected for premium or showcase venues. See the full comparison guide.

Can a roof be added to a panoramic padel court?

Yes. Selected fixed or retractable padel court roof systems can be integrated into panoramic padel court projects. Roof design requires separate structural review based on court layout, wind loads, site dimensions, drainage and local building requirements.

What information is needed for a quotation?

A project quotation typically requires the project country, number of courts, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor application, foundation condition, roof requirements, target timeline and preferably site photos or drawings. These details help determine the most suitable court and project configuration.

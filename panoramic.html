Buyer FAQ

Panoramic padel court procurement questions.

What is a panoramic padel court?

A panoramic padel court is designed to provide open spectator visibility through large tempered-glass viewing areas with reduced visual obstruction compared with more traditional framed layouts.

What should buyers compare between panoramic padel court manufacturers?

Compare steel specifications, corrosion protection, 12 mm tempered glass, welded mesh, turf, lighting, technical drawings, foundation interfaces, quality documentation, packing, shipping, installation guidance and project references on the same written specification.

Does SPORTENVO provide technical drawings and installation guidance?

Yes. Technical drawings, assembly information and installation guidance can be included in the confirmed project scope. Local civil work, lifting, approvals and on-site responsibilities should be defined before shipment.

Can the panoramic court be customized?

Project finish, color, turf, lighting and selected interfaces can be customized within the confirmed engineering and production scope. Structural changes require technical review rather than cosmetic approval only.

What information is needed before production?

Confirm court quantity, project location, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor use, foundation condition, access, wind / climate conditions, lighting, color and any roof or drainage interfaces.

How should I compare a low-price quotation with a complete quotation?

Check whether both quotations include the same glass, steel, mesh, coating, turf, lighting, hardware, packing, drawings, documentation and installation-support scope. A lower headline price may exclude project-critical items.

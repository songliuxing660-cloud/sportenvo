What Is a Panoramic Padel Court?

A panoramic padel court is a commercial court system designed to provide clear player and spectator visibility while maintaining a durable structural system. SPORTENVO panoramic courts are designed for clubs, resorts, sports facilities and multi-court commercial projects. The standard court system includes 12 mm tempered glass, a galvanized steel structure, professional padel turf and IP65 LED lighting as a complete set.

Panoramic vs Super Panoramic Padel Court

The Panoramic Court is designed for outdoor clubs and commercial venues where a professional, durable system is the priority. The Super Panoramic offers greater visual openness and is better suited to indoor venues and premium showcase projects.

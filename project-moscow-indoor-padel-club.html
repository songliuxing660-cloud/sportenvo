Seven-court indoor padel club project in Moscow

A large indoor commercial club built around a mixed court portfolio: three Super Panoramic courts, two Panoramic courts and two Single courts within a high-span indoor venue.

Project Challenges

What had to be solved before delivery.

01

High-use commercial operation

The venue was planned for frequent club use and competitive activity, increasing the importance of consistency and long-term structural reliability.

02

Mixed court portfolio

The project needed different court formats to support professional play, recreational use, training and efficient space utilisation.

03

Cross-border delivery

Packaging, shipping lists, documentation and installation sequencing had to be coordinated for international delivery.

Buyer Takeaways

Use this project as a planning reference.

Start with the operating model.

Court quantity, court mix and covered / uncovered areas should reflect bookings, coaching, events and the target customer experience.

Define the site interfaces.

Confirm roof clearance, ground or slab condition, access, drainage, utilities and local installation responsibilities before manufacturing.

Compare the complete scope.

Evaluate courts, technical documents, packing, logistics and installation support together rather than comparing a single headline equipment price.

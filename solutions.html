Buyer FAQ

Commercial padel solution questions.

Which padel court system is best for a commercial club?

There is no single best system for every club. Panoramic and Super Panoramic are often considered when viewing and presentation matter, while Classic can suit practical operation. Foundation, climate, budget, site geometry and operating model should determine the final choice.

What is the difference between Mobile Court and Modular Foundation?

Mobile Padel Court is the court-system direction for relocatable projects. Modular Foundation is the engineered base system that can support selected flexible-site installations. They can be used together, but they are not the same product.

Should I choose an Electric Tent or a permanent Roof?

Choose based on operating needs. Electric Tent provides retractable open / closed weather protection, while a permanent Roof is a separate structural solution for continuous coverage. Wind, drainage, clear height, foundation and local approvals must be reviewed for either option.

What should be reviewed for a coastal or high-wind project?

Start with the exact location, terrain and exposure, applicable local wind basis, foundation load path, anchors, corrosion protection and any roof condition. High-wind suitability cannot be confirmed from a product name alone.

Can SPORTENVO recommend a system from photos only?

Photos are useful, but they are not enough for final selection. Site dimensions, ground or structural information, drainage, access, climate, court quantity and operating requirements should also be provided.

What happens after I submit a project brief?

The project team can use the brief to narrow the suitable court, foundation and weather-protection direction, identify missing site information and prepare the next technical and commercial review steps.

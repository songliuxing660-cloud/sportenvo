Engineering Scope

Coordinate the system, not isolated components.

A commercial padel project works best when the court, supporting structure and site interfaces are reviewed together before production.

A

Court Structure

Select the court system around venue type, visibility, operating model and site exposure.

B

Foundation Strategy

Review concrete, existing slab, modular or elevated options against the actual ground condition.

C

Roof & Weather

Coordinate fixed, retractable or open-air solutions with clearance, drainage and local conditions.

D

Technical Drawings

Define dimensions, interfaces, anchors and installation information for the confirmed project scope.

E

Packing & Logistics

Plan export packing, component identification and shipment around the destination and site access.

F

Installation Guidance

Clarify supplier guidance, local labor, lifting equipment, tools and site responsibilities before delivery.

Project Inputs

Engineering starts with the site.

Before a final specification is confirmed, the project team needs enough site information to define the correct supply boundary and identify local responsibilities.

01 · Location

Country, city and destination requirements.

02 · Venue

Indoor, outdoor, rooftop, club, resort or sports facility.

03 · Site Dimensions

Available footprint, clear height and surrounding circulation.

04 · Ground / Structure

Existing slab, soil condition, rooftop structure or level differences.

05 · Environment

Wind exposure, coastal conditions, rainfall and weather requirements.

06 · Scope

Court count, foundation, roof, delivery and installation support.

For covered projects, review roof construction, fixed vs retractable systems, aluminum-alloy retractable configurations, foundation loads, drainage and wind before moving to the commercial roof system and project quotation.

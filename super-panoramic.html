What Is a Super Panoramic Padel Court?

A super panoramic padel court is a commercial padel court designed to provide a more open viewing experience around the glass playing enclosure. Compared with a standard panoramic court, the super panoramic configuration places greater emphasis on visual openness and premium court presentation, making it particularly suitable for high-end clubs, indoor venues, resorts and showcase projects.

SPORTENVO combines 12 mm tempered glass, professional steel structure, artificial turf and LED lighting within a complete commercial court system. Final configuration depends on the venue, foundation, structural requirements and project environment.

Design Concept

Designed for a More Open Court Experience

The Super Panoramic system is designed around clearer visual openness and a cleaner court appearance. This can be especially valuable where the court is part of a premium club environment, spectator viewing, indoor sports architecture or hotel and resort facilities.

Open-view design must still work together with structural stability, glass fixing, court geometry, venue conditions and foundation. Visual openness does not reduce the requirement for project-specific structural engineering.

12 mm Tempered Glass for Professional Padel Courts

SPORTENVO Super Panoramic courts use 12 mm tempered glass as part of the playing enclosure. The glass provides the transparent court wall system while supporting the visual openness expected from a premium panoramic configuration. Structure

Professional Steel Structure

The Super Panoramic court uses a professional Q235B steel structure integrated with glass, mesh and court components. Structural design should consider indoor or outdoor installation, site exposure, foundation, court layout, local requirements and optional roof system.

For projects in coastal or high-wind environments, the FORCE-HX high-wind padel court with a reinforced 200 × 200 × 6 mm structure should be evaluated separately.

Foundation

What Foundation Does a Super Panoramic Court Need?

Explore Modular Foundation →

A Super Panoramic padel court requires a stable and level foundation designed to support the complete court system. Permanent commercial projects commonly use a suitable concrete or paved base. For selected leased sites or projects requiring greater site flexibility, a modular steel foundation may be evaluated after reviewing site conditions and structural requirements.

Roof Solutions

Can a Super Panoramic Padel Court Have a Roof?

Explore Padel Court Roof Systems →

Yes. A Super Panoramic court can be integrated with selected fixed or retractable roof systems when the complete project is engineered accordingly. Roof planning must consider court layout, clearance, roof columns, foundation, wind loads, drainage, lighting and local regulations. The roof, court and foundation must be reviewed as one coordinated project.

FAQ

Super Panoramic Padel Court FAQ

What is a Super Panoramic padel court?

A Super Panoramic padel court is a commercial court system designed to provide a more open visual presentation around the glass playing enclosure. It is commonly considered for premium clubs, indoor venues, resorts and showcase projects where spectator visibility and court appearance are important.

What is the difference between Panoramic and Super Panoramic?

Both use tempered glass to provide clear viewing around the playing area, but the Super Panoramic places greater emphasis on visual openness. Standard Panoramic courts are widely used for commercial projects, while Super Panoramic systems are often selected for premium or showcase venues. Read the full comparison guide →

Is a Super Panoramic court suitable for indoor use?

Yes. Super Panoramic courts can be well suited to indoor commercial facilities because their open visual design integrates effectively with modern sports venues. Indoor projects should still review clear height, lighting, ventilation, foundation and local building requirements.

Can a Super Panoramic court be installed outdoors?

Yes. Super Panoramic courts can be used for selected outdoor commercial projects. Structural configuration, steel treatment, foundation, site exposure and local wind requirements should be reviewed. For high-wind environments, consider the FORCE-HX high-wind court.

What glass is used in a Super Panoramic court?

SPORTENVO Super Panoramic courts use 12 mm tempered glass as the playing enclosure wall system. The glass provides clear visibility while integrating with the steel court structure and approved fixing system.

Can a Super Panoramic court have a roof?

Yes. Selected fixed or retractable roof systems can be integrated into a Super Panoramic project. The roof, court, foundation and structural loads must be reviewed together according to the project location and local requirements.

What foundation does a Super Panoramic court need?

A stable and level foundation designed for the court structure is required. Permanent projects commonly use a suitable concrete or paved foundation. A modular steel foundation may be considered for selected projects after engineering review.

What information is needed for a Super Panoramic quotation?

A quotation typically requires the project country, number of courts, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor application, existing foundation, roof requirements, target timeline and preferably site photos or drawings.

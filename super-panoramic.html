Quick answers.

Ask the project team ↗
What changes compared with Panoramic?

Super Panoramic emphasizes reduced structural obstruction around viewing areas. Compare both systems with the same turf, lighting and delivery scope before choosing.

Can it be installed outdoors?

Selected outdoor installations are possible after review of site exposure, wind requirements, steel treatment and foundation. Share the location before confirming the model.

Can the appearance be customized?

Discuss frame colors, turf and lighting with your venue plan. Available options and their costs must be confirmed in the quotation.

What installation support is available?

Technical drawings and installation guidance can be coordinated with the supply scope. Confirm local installer responsibilities and any separately quoted on-site support.

What warranty terms apply?

Request written coverage, exclusions and the claim process for the quoted components before placing the order.

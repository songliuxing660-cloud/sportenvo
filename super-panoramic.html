Design Concept

Open Sightlines and Spectator Experience

The Super Panoramic system is designed around clearer visual openness and a cleaner court appearance. This can be especially valuable where the court is part of a premium club environment, spectator viewing, indoor sports architecture or hotel and resort facilities.

Open-view design must still work together with structural stability, glass fixing, court geometry, venue conditions and foundation. Visual openness does not reduce the requirement for project-specific structural engineering.

Glass System

12 mm Tempered Glass for Professional Padel Courts

Quality & Certifications →

SPORTENVO Super Panoramic courts use 12 mm tempered glass as part of the playing enclosure. The glass provides the transparent court wall system while supporting the visual openness expected from a premium panoramic configuration. Installation, fixing and glass layout must follow the approved technical design and local project requirements.

Structure

Professional Steel Structure

The Super Panoramic court uses a professional Q235B steel structure integrated with glass, mesh and court components. Structural design should consider indoor or outdoor installation, site exposure, foundation, court layout, local requirements and optional roof system.

For projects in coastal or high-wind environments, the FORCE-HX high-wind padel court with a reinforced 200 × 200 × 6 mm structure should be evaluated separately.

Foundation

What Foundation Does a Super Panoramic Court Need?

Explore Modular Foundation →

A Super Panoramic padel court requires a stable and level foundation designed to support the complete court system. Permanent commercial projects commonly use a suitable concrete or paved base. For selected leased sites or projects requiring greater site flexibility, a modular steel foundation may be evaluated after reviewing site conditions and structural requirements.

Roof Solutions

Can a Super Panoramic Padel Court Have a Roof?

Explore Padel Court Roof Systems →

Yes. A Super Panoramic court can be integrated with selected fixed or retractable roof systems when the complete project is engineered accordingly. Roof planning must consider court layout, clearance, roof columns, foundation, wind loads, drainage, lighting and local regulations. The roof, court and foundation must be reviewed as one coordinated project.

FAQ

Super Panoramic Padel Court FAQ

How much does a Super Panoramic court cost?

Pricing depends on the confirmed configuration, court quantity, customization and delivery scope. Send the project country, court count and site details for an itemized quotation. Compare equipment prices separately from freight, foundations and local installation.

How should I plan production and delivery?

Share your target opening date and destination at the enquiry stage. Confirm production time after specification approval, then allow separately for shipping, customs, site preparation and installation. The project schedule needs to cover each stage.

What warranty and installation support should I confirm?

Request the written warranty terms for the quoted structure, glass, turf and lighting, including coverage, exclusions and the claim process. Confirm the scope of drawings and installation guidance, local installer responsibilities, and whether on-site support is included or separately quoted.

What is a Super Panoramic Padel Court?

A Super Panoramic padel court is a commercial court system designed to provide a more open visual presentation around the glass playing enclosure. It is commonly considered for premium clubs, indoor venues, resorts and showcase projects where spectator visibility and court appearance are important.

What is the difference between Panoramic and Super Panoramic Padel Courts?

Both use tempered glass to provide clear viewing around the playing area, but the Super Panoramic places greater emphasis on visual openness and reduced structural obstruction. Standard Panoramic courts are widely used for commercial club and outdoor projects, while Super Panoramic systems are often selected for premium clubs, indoor facilities and showcase venues. Read the full comparison guide →

Why do premium clubs choose Super Panoramic courts?

Premium clubs choose Super Panoramic courts when visual openness, court aesthetics, spectator experience and premium venue positioning are important considerations. The more open sightlines can support better spectator visibility, photography and video, and contribute to a premium club atmosphere. Final selection depends on venue concept, budget and structural requirements.

Is a Super Panoramic court suitable for outdoor installation?

Yes. Super Panoramic courts can be used for selected outdoor commercial projects. Structural configuration, steel treatment, foundation, site exposure and local wind requirements should be reviewed. For high-wind environments, consider the FORCE-HX high-wind court.

What glass is used in the court?

SPORTENVO Super Panoramic courts use 12 mm tempered glass as the playing enclosure wall system. The glass provides clear visibility while integrating with the steel court structure and approved fixing system.

Can the court color, turf and lighting be customized?

Yes. Project-specific colors, turf options and lighting configurations can be discussed during the quotation process. Customization options depend on project requirements, quantities and confirmed specifications.

Can SPORTENVO support installation and project planning?

SPORTENVO provides technical drawings and installation guidance as part of the project supply scope. Court configuration, foundation and optional roof planning can be coordinated through the project review process. On-site installation support depends on project location and scope.

What information is required before quotation?

A quotation typically requires the project country, number of courts, site dimensions, indoor or outdoor application, existing foundation condition, roof requirements, target timeline and preferably site photos or drawings.
